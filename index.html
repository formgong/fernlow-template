The practice

Fernlow is a small practice of four therapists in a converted print shop off Alder Street. We see adults and couples who feel stuck, anxious, worn out or just not quite themselves, and we go at a pace that feels safe.

Step-free rooms with a garden door, so you can arrive and leave without walking through a waiting room if you'd rather not.

Neurodivergent-friendly. We send a short plan before your first session, you can take breaks whenever you like, and online sessions work fine with the camera off.

Mornings, evenings and Saturdays, because life doesn't stop for a 2 pm appointment.

Four steps

How it works

No forms to fill in beforehand and no pressure to commit. You can stop at any step.

  1. Step 1

    Book a free call

    Send the form or ring us. We'll find 15 minutes that suit you, by phone or video, and answer whatever you want to ask.

  2. Step 2

    Meet your therapist

    Your first session is about getting to know you. You don't need to know what to say. Working that out is part of our job.

  3. Step 3

    Find your rhythm

    Most people come weekly, some fortnightly. Every few weeks we look back together at how it's going and what to change.

  4. Step 4

    Finish when you're ready

    There's no minimum number of sessions. When you feel ready to stop, we plan a last session together so it ends well.

Questions

The things people usually ask before the first call.

Do I need a referral from a doctor?

No. You can contact us directly. If you're already seeing a doctor or psychiatrist, you're welcome to tell us on the intro call, but it isn't required.

What happens on the free intro call?

It's 15 minutes by phone or video. We ask what's bringing you to therapy in a sentence or two, explain how we work and answer your questions. Then you decide, with no follow-up emails if you'd rather not.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between you and your therapist. The rare exceptions, such as a serious risk to someone's safety, are explained in plain words in your first session.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you. Some people come for six sessions, others for a year or longer. We review together every few weeks, and you can stop whenever you want.

Can I switch between the room and online?

Yes, at any time and for any reason. Just let your therapist know before the session.

Is Fernlow an emergency service?

No. We can't respond to urgent situations and we don't monitor messages out of hours. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
